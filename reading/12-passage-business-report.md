## Quarterly Performance Summary: Customer Support Department

**Executive Summary**

This report summarizes the performance of the Customer Support Department for Q2, covering ticket volume, resolution time, and customer satisfaction scores. Overall, the department handled a higher volume of tickets than the previous quarter while maintaining service quality, though average resolution time increased slightly due to staffing constraints in May.

**Ticket Volume**

The department received 14,320 support tickets in Q2, an increase of 18% compared to Q1's 12,140 tickets. This rise is largely attributed to the launch of the new mobile app in April, which introduced several features that, while popular, generated a higher-than-expected number of user questions during the first few weeks after release.

**Resolution Time**

Average resolution time rose from 6.2 hours in Q1 to 7.8 hours in Q2. This increase coincided with two team members going on extended leave in May, reducing available staff by roughly 15% during that period. Despite this, the team avoided a more severe slowdown by temporarily reallocating two agents from the sales support team.

**Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)**

Despite the increase in resolution time, customer satisfaction scores remained stable at 4.3 out of 5, only slightly down from 4.4 in Q1. Exit surveys suggest that customers valued the clarity of communication during wait times — many agents proactively updated customers on delays, which appears to have offset frustration from longer response times.

**Recommendations**

Based on these findings, we recommend the following for Q3:

1. Hire two additional support agents before the next major product launch to prevent similar staffing shortfalls.
2. Expand the self-service help center, as data shows nearly 30% of Q2 tickets could have been resolved through existing documentation, had customers been aware of it.
3. Continue the practice of proactive delay communication, given its apparent positive impact on customer satisfaction despite longer wait times.

## Ghi chú đọc báo cáo

- Executive Summary luôn là phần nên đọc kỹ nhất nếu không có thời gian đọc toàn bộ — tóm tắt đủ các ý chính.
- Chú ý các cụm thể hiện quan hệ nhân quả: "attributed to", "coincided with", "resulting in" — đây thường là nội dung bị hỏi trong câu hỏi đọc hiểu.
- Phần "Recommendations" thường là phần quan trọng nhất về mặt hành động — đọc kỹ nếu mục đích đọc là để RA QUYẾT ĐỊNH, không chỉ để biết thông tin.
