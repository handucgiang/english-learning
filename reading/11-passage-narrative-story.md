# Bài đọc: Truyện ngắn (Narrative Fiction)

## The Last Train

Hoa had run the last three blocks, her bag banging against her hip with every step, but she already knew, somehow, that she was going to be too late. The station clock read 11:47 as she burst through the doors — thirteen minutes past the departure time printed on her ticket.

The platform was empty except for an older man sweeping cigarette butts into a dustpan, unbothered by her arrival. She checked the departure board twice, as if the information might change if she looked hard enough. It didn't. The 11:34 to Đà Lạt had already left.

She sat down on a cold metal bench, catching her breath, and pulled out her phone. Six missed calls from her mother. She didn't call back right away. Instead, she sat there for a moment, staring at the empty tracks, feeling strangely calm rather than panicked — an odd sort of relief mixed in with the disappointment.

It wasn't until she was halfway through composing an apology text to her mother that she realized why. She hadn't actually wanted to go. Not really. The trip had been her mother's idea — a family reunion with relatives Hoa barely remembered, in a town she associated mostly with obligation rather than warmth. Missing the train wasn't a disaster. It was, if she was honest with herself, something close to permission.

She deleted the apology text and typed a new one instead: "Missed the train. I think I'm going to stay in the city this weekend, actually. I'll visit next month, I promise — just need some time to myself."

She hit send before she could second-guess it, and for the first time all week, she felt like she could breathe.

## Ghi chú kỹ thuật kể chuyện (liên hệ với [speaking/10-storytelling-techniques.md](../speaking/10-storytelling-techniques.md))

- Câu mở đầu đặt ngay vào tình huống căng thẳng (in medias res) — không mất thời gian giới thiệu dài dòng.
- Chi tiết nhỏ ("cigarette butts", "cold metal bench") tạo bối cảnh sống động mà không cần mô tả dài dòng.
- Bước ngoặt cảm xúc ẩn trong hành động (xóa tin nhắn xin lỗi, viết lại tin nhắn khác) thay vì nói thẳng "Hoa quyết định không đi nữa."

## Từ vựng nổi bật

| Từ/cụm | Nghĩa |
|---|---|
| burst through (the doors) | lao qua, xông vào |
| departure board | bảng thông báo giờ khởi hành |
| catch one's breath | lấy lại hơi thở |
| odd sort of relief | một cảm giác nhẹ nhõm kỳ lạ |
| second-guess (oneself) | tự nghi ngờ/đắn đo lại quyết định của mình |
| obligation | nghĩa vụ, trách nhiệm (phải làm dù không muốn) |

## Câu hỏi đọc hiểu

1. What happened when Hoa arrived at the station?
2. How did Hoa feel immediately after realizing she missed the train?
3. (Suy luận) Why does the passage mention "six missed calls from her mother"?
4. (Suy luận) What does Hoa's choice to delete the apology text and write a different message suggest about her true feelings toward the trip?
5. What does the final sentence ("she felt like she could breathe") suggest about Hoa's emotional state throughout the story?

<details>
<summary>Đáp án</summary>

1. She missed her train — it had already departed 13 minutes before she arrived.
2. She felt strangely calm, with a mix of relief and disappointment, rather than pure panic or distress.
3. It suggests her mother had been anxious/eager for her to make the trip, hinting at pressure or expectation from her mother — which becomes relevant to understanding why Hoa feels relief later.
4. It suggests she didn't genuinely want to go on the trip — missing the train gave her an unconscious sense of "permission" not to go, which she only realizes partway through writing the apology.
5. It suggests she had been feeling burdened or stressed (likely by the obligation of the family trip) before this moment, and the decision to stay brought a sense of relief and freedom.

</details>
