# Bài đọc nâng cao: Bài viết Châm biếm (Satirical Article)

Áp dụng kỹ thuật nhận diện satire từ [18-satire-irony-figurative-language.md](18-satire-irony-figurative-language.md) trước khi đọc. Nhắc lại: satire trình bày điều phi lý một cách "nghiêm túc" để phê phán một vấn đề thực tế.

## Local Office Achieves Full Productivity by Scheduling Meeting About Reducing Meetings

In a groundbreaking development being hailed internally as "a real turning point," the marketing department at a mid-sized firm has announced a 90-minute meeting next Thursday to discuss strategies for reducing the number of unnecessary meetings.

"We felt like something needed to change," said department head Trevor Lin, speaking from inside a conference room he had personally booked for the next four hours to prepare his opening remarks. "Our calendars are just back-to-back meetings. It's honestly become a real challenge to find time to talk about it properly."

The meeting will reportedly include a 20-minute icebreaker, a PowerPoint presentation titled "Why We Meet Too Much," and a breakout session in which employees will be divided into smaller groups to discuss the presentation before reconvening to share their group's conclusions with the full team.

A follow-up meeting has already been scheduled for the following week to review action items from the first meeting, with a tentative agenda item to "discuss whether the meeting-reduction meeting achieved its goals."

Not everyone is convinced. "I got the calendar invite while I was in another meeting about our meeting culture," said one employee, who asked to remain anonymous. "I didn't have time to open it because I was already fifteen minutes late for a meeting about being on time to meetings."

At press time, three additional employees had proposed forming a committee to oversee the implementation of whatever gets decided in Thursday's meeting, pending approval at next month's steering committee meeting.

## Phân tích satire trong bài

| Chi tiết | Điều đang bị châm biếm |
|---|---|
| Họp để bàn về việc giảm họp | Nghịch lý/tự mâu thuẫn — chính giải pháp lại là vấn đề |
| "reportedly include a 20-minute icebreaker" trong cuộc họp VỀ việc giảm họp | Phê phán văn hóa họp hành rườm rà, hình thức |
| Đã lên lịch họp tiếp theo để "đánh giá cuộc họp giảm họp" | Đẩy sự phi lý lên cao trào — vòng lặp không hồi kết |
| "three additional employees had proposed forming a committee" | Châm biếm xu hướng quan liêu hóa mọi vấn đề bằng ủy ban/quy trình |

## Từ vựng nổi bật

| Từ/cụm | Nghĩa |
|---|---|
| groundbreaking | mang tính đột phá (thường dùng nghiêm túc — ở đây dùng mỉa mai cho việc tầm thường) |
| icebreaker | hoạt động phá băng, làm quen đầu buổi họp |
| breakout session | phiên thảo luận nhóm nhỏ |
| reconvene | họp lại, tập hợp lại |
| action items | các việc cần làm sau cuộc họp |
| steering committee | ủy ban chỉ đạo/điều hành |

## Câu hỏi đọc hiểu

1. What is ironic about the main event described in the article?
2. What does the length of Trevor Lin's meeting preparation ("booked... for the next four hours") suggest, given the topic of the meeting?
3. What is being satirized by the scheduling of a follow-up meeting to review the first meeting?
4. (Suy luận) What is the article's overall message about corporate meeting culture?

<details>
<summary>Đáp án</summary>

1. The meeting's purpose is to reduce unnecessary meetings, yet the article shows this "solution" adding even more meetings — a self-defeating irony.
2. It suggests the excessive, unproductive nature of meeting culture — spending four hours preparing for a 90-minute meeting undercuts the stated goal of efficiency.
3. It satirizes the tendency of corporate culture to respond to problems with more process/meetings rather than actual solutions, creating an endless loop.
4. The article satirizes how corporate culture often responds to inefficiency (too many meetings) with more of the same behavior (more meetings, committees, and process), rather than genuine change.

</details>
