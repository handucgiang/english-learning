# Từ vựng: Công việc & văn phòng

| Từ / Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **deadline** | hạn chót | We have a *deadline* on Friday. |
| **workload** | khối lượng công việc | My *workload* has increased this month. |
| **colleague** | đồng nghiệp | I had lunch with a *colleague* today. |
| **supervisor** | người giám sát/quản lý trực tiếp | My *supervisor* reviewed my report. |
| **meeting minutes** | biên bản cuộc họp | Please send me the *meeting minutes*. |
| **overtime** | làm thêm giờ | He worked *overtime* to finish the project. |
| **promotion** | thăng chức | She got a *promotion* last year. |
| **resign** | từ chức | He decided to *resign* from his position. |
| **negotiate** | đàm phán | We need to *negotiate* the contract terms. |
| **assign a task** | giao việc | The manager *assigned* me a new *task*. |
| **collaborate** | hợp tác/phối hợp | Our teams *collaborate* on every project. |
| **feedback** | phản hồi/góp ý | I got useful *feedback* from my manager. |
| **prioritize** | ưu tiên | You should *prioritize* urgent tasks first. |
| **schedule a meeting** | đặt lịch họp | Can you *schedule a meeting* for tomorrow? |
| **on track** | đúng tiến độ | The project is *on track* for launch. |

## Bài tập nhỏ

Chọn từ đúng để hoàn thành câu:

1. Our project is behind schedule, so we need to work ______ this week. (deadline / overtime)
2. I got great ______ from my manager after the presentation. (feedback / resign)
3. Please ______ these tasks by importance. (prioritize / negotiate)
4. She was given a ______ after two years of great performance. (promotion / workload)
5. Let's ______ a meeting to discuss the budget. (schedule / assign)
