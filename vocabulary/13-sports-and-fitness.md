# Từ vựng: Thể thao & Rèn luyện sức khỏe

| Từ / Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **work out** | tập thể dục | I *work out* three times a week. |
| **stamina** | sức bền | Running builds *stamina* over time. |
| **stretch** | giãn cơ | Always *stretch* before exercising. |
| **warm up / cool down** | khởi động / thả lỏng | Don't skip the *warm-up* before a workout. |
| **sore (muscles)** | đau nhức (cơ) | My legs are *sore* after yesterday's run. |
| **coach** | huấn luyện viên | Our *coach* pushes us to improve every session. |
| **opponent** | đối thủ | She easily defeated her *opponent*. |
| **referee** | trọng tài | The *referee* made a controversial call. |
| **score a goal/point** | ghi bàn/điểm | He *scored the winning goal* in the final minute. |
| **draw (a match)** | hòa (trận đấu) | The two teams *drew* 1-1. |
| **championship** | giải vô địch | They won the national *championship*. |
| **injury** | chấn thương | An *injury* kept him out for the season. |
| **personal best** | thành tích cá nhân tốt nhất | She set a *personal best* in the marathon. |
| **endurance** | sức chịu đựng, độ bền | Marathon training builds *endurance*. |
| **spectator** | khán giả | Thousands of *spectators* filled the stadium. |

## Bài tập nhỏ

1. Remember to ______ before you start running, or you'll get injured.
2. He suffered a serious ______ and couldn't play the rest of the season.
3. The match ended in a ______ , 2-2.
4. She trains every day to build up her ______ for the marathon.
5. The ______ disagreed with the referee's decision.
