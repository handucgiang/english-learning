# Từ vựng: Thời tiết & Thiên nhiên

| Từ / Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **forecast** | dự báo thời tiết | The *forecast* predicts rain tomorrow. |
| **humid** | ẩm (thời tiết) | Summers here are hot and *humid*. |
| **drizzle** | mưa phùn | It was just a light *drizzle*, not heavy rain. |
| **downpour** | mưa lớn, mưa như trút | We got caught in a sudden *downpour*. |
| **thunderstorm** | giông bão (sấm sét) | A *thunderstorm* is expected this evening. |
| **overcast** | u ám, nhiều mây | The sky was *overcast* all morning. |
| **breeze** | gió nhẹ | A cool *breeze* came off the ocean. |
| **gale / gust** | gió mạnh / cơn gió giật | A strong *gust* of wind knocked over the umbrella. |
| **drought** | hạn hán | The region is suffering from a severe *drought*. |
| **flood** | lũ lụt | Heavy rain caused *flooding* in the city. |
| **climate change** | biến đổi khí hậu | *Climate change* is affecting weather patterns worldwide. |
| **ecosystem** | hệ sinh thái | Coral reefs support a rich *ecosystem*. |
| **endangered species** | loài có nguy cơ tuyệt chủng | Tigers are an *endangered species*. |
| **wildlife** | động vật hoang dã | The reserve protects local *wildlife*. |
| **sustainable** | bền vững | The company adopted more *sustainable* practices. |

## Bài tập nhỏ

1. Bring an umbrella — the ______ says it'll rain all day.
2. The sky has been ______ since this morning, no sun at all.
3. Pandas are an ______ species.
4. The region hasn't had rain in months — it's facing a severe ______.
5. The company is trying to become more ______ by reducing waste.

<details>
<summary>Đáp án (bấm để xem)</summary>

1. forecast
2. overcast
3. endangered
4. drought
5. sustainable

</details>
