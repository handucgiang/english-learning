# Từ vựng: AI & Công nghệ mới nổi

| Từ / Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **machine learning** | học máy | The system uses *machine learning* to improve over time. |
| **neural network** | mạng nơ-ron | The model is based on a deep *neural network*. |
| **algorithm bias** | thiên vị thuật toán | Researchers are studying *algorithm bias* in hiring tools. |
| **automation** | tự động hóa | *Automation* is changing many manufacturing jobs. |
| **chatbot** | trợ lý ảo trò chuyện | Customer support now uses a *chatbot* for quick questions. |
| **generative AI** | AI tạo sinh | *Generative AI* can create text, images, and even music. |
| **data set** | tập dữ liệu | The model was trained on a huge *data set*. |
| **virtual reality (VR)** | thực tế ảo | The game uses *virtual reality* headsets. |
| **augmented reality (AR)** | thực tế tăng cường | The app uses *augmented reality* to show furniture in your room. |
| **blockchain** | công nghệ chuỗi khối | *Blockchain* technology underlies most cryptocurrencies. |
| **Internet of Things (IoT)** | Internet vạn vật | Smart homes rely on *Internet of Things* devices. |
| **autonomous (vehicle)** | tự hành (xe) | The company is testing *autonomous* vehicles. |
| **breakthrough** | bước đột phá | Scientists announced a major *breakthrough* in the field. |
| **disrupt (an industry)** | làm thay đổi/xáo trộn (một ngành) | The technology could *disrupt* the entire industry. |
| **ethical concerns** | những lo ngại về đạo đức | There are growing *ethical concerns* around AI use. |

## Bài tập nhỏ

1. The company is developing ______ vehicles that don't need a human driver.
2. Many customer service teams now use a ______ to answer common questions.
3. The model was trained on a massive ______ of images.
4. There are increasing ______ about how AI is being used in hiring.
5. This new technology could ______ the entire taxi industry.

<details>
<summary>Đáp án (bấm để xem)</summary>

1. autonomous
2. chatbot
3. data set
4. ethical concerns
5. disrupt

</details>
