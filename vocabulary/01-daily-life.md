# Từ vựng: Đời sống hằng ngày

| Từ / Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **wake up** | thức dậy | I usually *wake up* at 6 a.m. |
| **household chores** | việc nhà | She does the *household chores* every weekend. |
| **commute** | đi lại (đi làm/học hằng ngày) | My *commute* to work takes about 30 minutes. |
| **grocery shopping** | đi chợ/siêu thị | We go *grocery shopping* every Sunday. |
| **run errands** | đi làm việc vặt | I need to *run errands* this afternoon. |
| **relax** | thư giãn | I like to *relax* by reading a book. |
| **habit** | thói quen | Drinking coffee in the morning is a *habit* of mine. |
| **routine** | thói quen/lịch trình cố định | Exercise is part of my daily *routine*. |
| **exhausted** | kiệt sức | I was *exhausted* after the long day. |
| **procrastinate** | trì hoãn | Stop *procrastinating* and finish your homework. |
| **tidy up** | dọn dẹp gọn gàng | Please *tidy up* your room before dinner. |
| **get along with** | hòa hợp với | I *get along with* my neighbors very well. |
| **look forward to** | mong chờ | I'm *looking forward to* the weekend. |
| **catch up on** | làm cho kịp/bù lại | I need to *catch up on* sleep this weekend. |
| **take a break** | nghỉ giải lao | Let's *take a break* for 10 minutes. |

## Bài tập nhỏ

Điền từ thích hợp vào chỗ trống (dùng từ trong bảng trên):

1. I always ______ at 6:30 and go for a run.
2. My daily ______ includes coffee, work, and a walk in the evening.
3. She was so ______ after the exam that she slept for 12 hours.
4. We need to do some ______ before the fridge is empty.
5. I'm really ______ the trip next month.

<details>
<summary>Đáp án (bấm để xem)</summary>

1. wake up
2. routine
3. exhausted
4. grocery shopping
5. looking forward to
