# Từ vựng: Internet & Mạng xã hội

| Từ / Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **browse (the web)** | lướt web | I like to *browse the web* before bed. |
| **search engine** | công cụ tìm kiếm | Google is the most popular *search engine*. |
| **broadband** | mạng băng thông rộng | We upgraded to a faster *broadband* connection. |
| **Wi-Fi signal** | tín hiệu wifi | The *Wi-Fi signal* is weak in this room. |
| **buffer / buffering** | tải chậm, giật (video) | The video keeps *buffering* on slow connections. |
| **feed (social media)** | bảng tin | My *feed* is full of vacation photos lately. |
| **post / share** | đăng bài / chia sẻ | She *posted* a photo from her trip. |
| **like / comment** | thích / bình luận | The post got hundreds of *likes* and *comments*. |
| **follower** | người theo dõi | He has over 10,000 *followers* on Instagram. |
| **hashtag** | thẻ gắn từ khóa | The campaign used a catchy *hashtag*. |
| **algorithm** | thuật toán | The *algorithm* decides what content you see. |
| **notification** | thông báo | I turned off *notifications* to focus better. |
| **screenshot** | chụp màn hình | Can you send me a *screenshot* of the error? |
| **trending** | đang thịnh hành | This topic is *trending* right now. |
| **doomscrolling** | lướt mạng liên tục nội dung tiêu cực | I caught myself *doomscrolling* the news again. |

## Bài tập nhỏ

1. My internet connection is too slow, the video keeps ______.
2. She has thousands of ______ on social media.
3. The topic started ______ after the announcement.
4. Turn off ______ if you want to focus without distractions.
5. Could you send me a ______ of that error message?
