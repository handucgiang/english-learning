# Từ vựng: Nghệ thuật, Giải trí & Truyền thông

| Từ / Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **plot** | cốt truyện | The movie's *plot* was a bit predictable. |
| **cast** | dàn diễn viên | The film has an impressive *cast*. |
| **soundtrack** | nhạc phim | I loved the *soundtrack* of that movie. |
| **box office** | doanh thu phòng vé | The film topped the *box office* this week. |
| **rave review** | bài đánh giá khen ngợi | The play received *rave reviews* from critics. |
| **binge-watch** | xem liên tục nhiều tập | We *binge-watched* the whole series in a weekend. |
| **subscription** | dịch vụ đăng ký (trả phí) | I have a *subscription* to a streaming service. |
| **exhibit / exhibition** | triển lãm | The museum is hosting a new art *exhibition*. |
| **masterpiece** | kiệt tác | The painting is considered a *masterpiece*. |
| **critically acclaimed** | được giới phê bình đánh giá cao | It's a *critically acclaimed* documentary. |
| **headline** | tiêu đề (tin tức) | The scandal made *headlines* worldwide. |
| **broadcast** | phát sóng | The event will be *broadcast* live. |
| **go viral** | lan truyền nhanh (trên mạng) | The video *went viral* within hours. |
| **influencer** | người có sức ảnh hưởng (mạng xã hội) | Many brands work with social media *influencers*. |
| **censorship** | kiểm duyệt | The film faced *censorship* in some countries. |

## Bài tập nhỏ

1. The movie has an amazing ______, but the ending felt rushed.
2. We ______ the entire series in just two days.
3. The video ______ overnight and got millions of views.
4. The exhibition featured several ______ from famous artists.
5. The news ______ the story worldwide within hours.

<details>
<summary>Đáp án (bấm để xem)</summary>

1. plot
2. binge-watched
3. went viral
4. masterpieces
5. headlined *(hoặc: "made headlines")*

</details>
