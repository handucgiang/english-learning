# Từ vựng: Giao thông & Di chuyển

| Từ / Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **commute** | đi lại (đi làm/học hằng ngày) | My daily *commute* takes about 40 minutes. |
| **rush hour** | giờ cao điểm | Traffic is terrible during *rush hour*. |
| **traffic jam** | kẹt xe | We got stuck in a *traffic jam* for an hour. |
| **public transport** | phương tiện công cộng | I usually take *public transport* to work. |
| **fare** | giá vé | The bus *fare* increased last month. |
| **platform** | sân ga, sảnh đợi | The train departs from *platform* 3. |
| **transfer (trains/buses)** | chuyển tuyến | You'll need to *transfer* at the next station. |
| **detour** | đường vòng, đường tránh | We had to take a *detour* because of roadwork. |
| **roadwork** | công trình sửa đường | *Roadwork* is causing delays on the highway. |
| **pedestrian** | người đi bộ | Drivers must yield to *pedestrians*. |
| **intersection** | ngã tư, giao lộ | The accident happened at a busy *intersection*. |
| **speed limit** | tốc độ giới hạn | He was fined for exceeding the *speed limit*. |
| **breakdown (vehicle)** | xe bị hỏng dọc đường | Our car had a *breakdown* on the highway. |
| **carpool** | đi chung xe | We *carpool* to save on gas. |
| **layover** | thời gian dừng chờ (khi bay) | We have a two-hour *layover* in Singapore. |

## Bài tập nhỏ

1. I try to avoid driving during ______ because the roads are so crowded.
2. You'll need to ______ to the blue line at the next stop.
3. Our flight has a three-hour ______ before the connecting flight.
4. The road is closed for ______, so we had to take a ______.
5. Always yield to ______ at crosswalks.

<details>
<summary>Đáp án (bấm để xem)</summary>

1. rush hour
2. transfer
3. layover
4. roadwork / detour
5. pedestrians

</details>
