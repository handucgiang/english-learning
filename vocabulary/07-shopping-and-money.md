# Từ vựng: Mua sắm & Tiền bạc

| Từ / Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **budget** | ngân sách | I'm trying to stick to a tight *budget* this month. |
| **discount** | giảm giá | This jacket is on *discount* — 20% off. |
| **refund** | hoàn tiền | I returned the item and got a full *refund*. |
| **receipt** | hóa đơn | Keep your *receipt* in case you need to return it. |
| **afford** | có khả năng chi trả | I can't *afford* a new car right now. |
| **installment** | trả góp | I'm paying for the phone in monthly *installments*. |
| **bargain** | món hời / mặc cả | This laptop was a real *bargain* at that price. |
| **overpriced** | (giá) quá đắt so với giá trị | That restaurant is *overpriced* for the portion size. |
| **savings** | tiền tiết kiệm | I put 10% of my salary into *savings* every month. |
| **worth it** | đáng giá | The trip was expensive, but it was *worth it*. |
| **spend on** | chi tiền vào | I spend a lot on groceries every week. |
| **cut back on** | cắt giảm chi tiêu | We're trying to *cut back on* eating out. |
| **in stock / out of stock** | còn hàng / hết hàng | Sorry, that size is currently *out of stock*. |
| **warranty** | bảo hành | This washing machine comes with a two-year *warranty*. |
| **loyalty program** | chương trình khách hàng thân thiết | I earn points through the store's *loyalty program*. |

## Bài tập nhỏ

1. I don't think I can ______ that expensive bag right now.
2. Please keep your ______ in case you want to exchange the item.
3. We're trying to ______ unnecessary spending this year.
4. The store offers a great ______ program — I get points every time I shop.
5. Unfortunately, this item is currently ______.
