# Từ vựng: Du lịch

| Từ / Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **itinerary** | lịch trình chuyến đi | Here is our *itinerary* for the trip. |
| **book a flight** | đặt vé máy bay | I need to *book a flight* to Tokyo. |
| **layover** | thời gian chờ nối chuyến | We have a 3-hour *layover* in Seoul. |
| **check in / check out** | nhận phòng / trả phòng | *Check-in* time is 2 p.m. |
| **luggage / baggage** | hành lý | My *luggage* was lost at the airport. |
| **customs** | hải quan | You must go through *customs* first. |
| **currency exchange** | đổi tiền | Where can I find a *currency exchange*? |
| **accommodation** | chỗ ở | We booked our *accommodation* online. |
| **sightseeing** | tham quan | We spent the day *sightseeing* downtown. |
| **souvenir** | quà lưu niệm | I bought a *souvenir* for my sister. |
| **jet lag** | mệt do lệch múi giờ | I had bad *jet lag* after the flight. |
| **local cuisine** | ẩm thực địa phương | We tried the *local cuisine* everywhere. |
| **tourist attraction** | điểm tham quan | This temple is a famous *tourist attraction*. |
| **travel insurance** | bảo hiểm du lịch | Always buy *travel insurance* before a trip. |
| **off the beaten path** | ít người biết đến/không phổ thông | We found a village *off the beaten path*. |

## Bài tập nhỏ

Điền từ thích hợp:

1. We need to buy ______ in case something happens during the trip.
2. Our flight has a 5-hour ______ in Singapore.
3. Don't forget to declare items at ______ when you arrive.
4. This market is a popular ______ for tourists.
5. I always feel tired from ______ after long flights.

<details>
<summary>Đáp án (bấm để xem)</summary>

1. travel insurance
2. layover
3. customs
4. tourist attraction
5. jet lag

</details>
