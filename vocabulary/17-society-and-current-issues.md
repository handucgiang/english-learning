# Từ vựng: Xã hội & Các vấn đề thời sự

| Từ / Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **inequality** | bất bình đẳng | The report highlights growing income *inequality*. |
| **poverty** | nghèo đói | The program aims to reduce *poverty* in rural areas. |
| **unemployment** | thất nghiệp | *Unemployment* rates rose during the recession. |
| **policy** | chính sách | The government introduced a new housing *policy*. |
| **legislation** | luật, pháp chế | New *legislation* was passed to protect workers. |
| **protest** | biểu tình, phản đối | Thousands joined the *protest* downtown. |
| **advocate (for)** | ủng hộ, vận động cho | She *advocates for* better healthcare access. |
| **discrimination** | phân biệt đối xử | The law prohibits *discrimination* in hiring. |
| **diversity** | sự đa dạng | The company values *diversity* in its workforce. |
| **awareness** | nhận thức, ý thức | The campaign raised public *awareness* about the issue. |
| **sustainability** | tính bền vững | Many companies now focus on *sustainability*. |
| **migration** | di cư | Climate change is driving *migration* in some regions. |
| **welfare** | phúc lợi xã hội | The country has a strong *welfare* system. |
| **grassroots (movement)** | phong trào từ cơ sở, quần chúng | It started as a *grassroots movement*. |
| **public opinion** | dư luận xã hội | *Public opinion* shifted after the scandal. |

## Bài tập nhỏ

1. The new law aims to reduce income ______ between rich and poor.
2. The campaign helped raise public ______ about mental health.
3. Thousands of workers joined the ______ against the new policy.
4. The organization ______ for equal access to education.
5. It started as a small ______ movement before gaining national attention.

<details>
<summary>Đáp án (bấm để xem)</summary>

1. inequality
2. awareness
3. protest
4. advocates
5. grassroots

</details>
