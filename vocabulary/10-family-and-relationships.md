# Từ vựng: Gia đình & Các mối quan hệ

| Từ / Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **sibling** | anh chị em ruột | I have two *siblings*, a brother and a sister. |
| **spouse** | vợ/chồng (trang trọng) | Employees may bring their *spouse* to the event. |
| **in-laws** | gia đình bên vợ/chồng | We're having dinner with my *in-laws* tonight. |
| **extended family** | đại gia đình (họ hàng) | Our *extended family* gathers every Tết. |
| **single parent** | cha/mẹ đơn thân | She's raising two kids as a *single parent*. |
| **raise (kids)** | nuôi dạy (con cái) | They *raised* three children in this house. |
| **get along (with)** | hòa hợp với | I *get along* really well with my in-laws. |
| **grow apart** | dần xa cách (trong quan hệ) | Over the years, the two friends *grew apart*. |
| **bond (with)** | gắn kết, gần gũi | Camping trips helped us *bond* as a family. |
| **estranged** | xa cách, ly tán (quan hệ) | He's been *estranged* from his father for years. |
| **reconcile** | hàn gắn, làm hòa | The siblings finally *reconciled* after years apart. |
| **dependent** | người phụ thuộc | She has two *dependents* — her kids. |
| **guardian** | người giám hộ | His aunt became his legal *guardian*. |
| **generation gap** | khoảng cách thế hệ | There's a noticeable *generation gap* in our family. |
| **nurture** | nuôi dưỡng, chăm sóc | Good parents *nurture* both confidence and discipline. |

## Bài tập nhỏ

1. My brother and sister are my only ______.
2. Despite living far apart, we still ______ well.
3. She became the children's legal ______ after the accident.
4. The two brothers were ______ for over a decade before reconciling.
5. It's important to ______ a child's curiosity, not just discipline.

<details>
<summary>Đáp án (bấm để xem)</summary>

1. siblings
2. get along
3. guardian
4. estranged
5. nurture

</details>
