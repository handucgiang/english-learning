# Từ vựng: Giáo dục & Học tập

| Từ / Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **enroll (in)** | ghi danh, đăng ký học | I *enrolled in* a coding course last month. |
| **tuition** | học phí | University *tuition* keeps rising every year. |
| **scholarship** | học bổng | She received a full *scholarship* to study abroad. |
| **curriculum** | chương trình học | The school updated its *curriculum* this year. |
| **assignment** | bài tập được giao | I have three *assignments* due this week. |
| **grade / GPA** | điểm số / điểm trung bình | He graduated with a high *GPA*. |
| **lecture** | bài giảng | The professor's *lecture* was really engaging. |
| **major (in)** | chuyên ngành | She's *majoring in* economics. |
| **degree** | bằng cấp | He holds a *degree* in engineering. |
| **plagiarism** | đạo văn | The university has strict rules against *plagiarism*. |
| **drop out (of)** | bỏ học giữa chừng | He *dropped out of* college to start a business. |
| **cram (for an exam)** | học dồn, nhồi nhét | I stayed up all night *cramming* for the exam. |
| **extracurricular activities** | hoạt động ngoại khóa | Joining clubs is a great *extracurricular activity*. |
| **literacy** | khả năng đọc viết | The program aims to improve *literacy* rates. |
| **self-taught** | tự học | He's a *self-taught* programmer. |

## Bài tập nhỏ

1. She had to ______ all night before the final exam.
2. He's ______ economics at university.
3. I need to finish this ______ before Friday.
4. Many students rely on ______ to afford tuition.
5. He decided to ______ college after one semester.

<details>
<summary>Đáp án (bấm để xem)</summary>

1. cram
2. majoring in
3. assignment
4. scholarships
5. drop out of

</details>
