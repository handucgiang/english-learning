# Từ vựng: Sức khỏe

| Từ / Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **symptom** | triệu chứng | A fever is a common *symptom* of the flu. |
| **prescription** | đơn thuốc | The doctor gave me a *prescription* for antibiotics. |
| **recover** | hồi phục | It took two weeks for him to *recover* from surgery. |
| **chronic** | mãn tính | She has a *chronic* back problem. |
| **come down with** | mắc (bệnh gì đó) | I think I'm *coming down with* a cold. |
| **work out** | tập thể dục | I try to *work out* three times a week. |
| **well-being** | sức khỏe tổng thể/hạnh phúc | Mental *well-being* is just as important as physical health. |
| **nutritious** | bổ dưỡng | She always tries to eat *nutritious* meals. |
| **immune system** | hệ miễn dịch | Getting enough sleep helps boost your *immune system*. |
| **stress relief** | giảm căng thẳng | Yoga is a great form of *stress relief*. |
| **checkup** | khám sức khỏe định kỳ | I have an annual *checkup* next week. |
| **allergic (to)** | dị ứng (với) | I'm *allergic to* peanuts. |
| **burn out** | kiệt sức (do làm việc quá sức) | He *burned out* after months of overtime. |
| **balanced diet** | chế độ ăn cân bằng | A *balanced diet* includes protein, carbs, and vegetables. |
| **get over (an illness)** | khỏi bệnh | It took her a while to *get over* the flu. |

## Bài tập nhỏ

1. I need to ______ my diet — too much fast food lately.
2. He's been feeling tired, so he thinks he's ______ something.
3. My doctor recommended a ______ diet with more vegetables.
4. I go for a medical ______ once a year.
5. After weeks of overtime, she completely ______.
