# Từ vựng: Kinh doanh & Tài chính

| Từ / Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **revenue** | doanh thu | The company's *revenue* grew by 15% this year. |
| **profit / loss** | lợi nhuận / thua lỗ | The business made a *profit* for the first time. |
| **investment** | khoản đầu tư | This is a long-term *investment*. |
| **shareholder** | cổ đông | *Shareholders* voted on the merger. |
| **startup** | công ty khởi nghiệp | He founded a tech *startup* two years ago. |
| **merger / acquisition** | sáp nhập / mua lại | The *merger* created the largest firm in the industry. |
| **stock market** | thị trường chứng khoán | The *stock market* dropped sharply today. |
| **inflation** | lạm phát | Rising *inflation* is affecting consumer spending. |
| **interest rate** | lãi suất | The central bank raised *interest rates* again. |
| **loan** | khoản vay | They took out a *loan* to expand the business. |
| **debt** | nợ | The company is trying to pay off its *debt*. |
| **cash flow** | dòng tiền | Poor *cash flow* is a common issue for small businesses. |
| **bankruptcy** | phá sản | The company filed for *bankruptcy* last year. |
| **supply and demand** | cung và cầu | Prices are determined by *supply and demand*. |
| **outsource** | thuê ngoài | They *outsource* customer support to another country. |

## Bài tập nhỏ

1. The company reported a huge ______ increase this quarter.
2. Many small businesses struggle with ______ , even when they're profitable on paper.
3. Rising ______ has made everyday goods more expensive.
4. The two companies announced a ______ last week.
5. They decided to ______ their IT support to cut costs.

<details>
<summary>Đáp án (bấm để xem)</summary>

1. revenue
2. cash flow
3. inflation
4. merger
5. outsource

</details>
