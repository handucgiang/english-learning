# Từ vựng: Cảm xúc & Tính cách

| Từ / Cụm từ | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| **overwhelmed** | choáng ngợp, quá tải | I feel *overwhelmed* with all this work. |
| **frustrated** | bực bội, thất vọng | She was *frustrated* with the slow internet. |
| **relieved** | nhẹ nhõm | I was *relieved* when the exam results came out. |
| **anxious** | lo lắng | He felt *anxious* before the interview. |
| **content** | hài lòng, mãn nguyện | She seems *content* with her current job. |
| **outgoing** | hướng ngoại, cởi mở | My sister is very *outgoing* and makes friends easily. |
| **reserved** | kín đáo, ít nói | He's quite *reserved* when meeting new people. |
| **easygoing** | dễ tính, thoải mái | My manager is very *easygoing* about deadlines. |
| **stubborn** | bướng bỉnh, cứng đầu | He's too *stubborn* to admit he's wrong. |
| **considerate** | chu đáo, biết quan tâm người khác | She's always *considerate* of others' feelings. |
| **ambitious** | tham vọng, có chí tiến thủ | He's very *ambitious* about his career. |
| **reliable** | đáng tin cậy | You can count on him — he's very *reliable*. |
| **upset** | buồn, khó chịu | She was *upset* about the cancelled trip. |
| **grateful** | biết ơn | I'm *grateful* for all your help. |
| **indifferent** | thờ ơ, không quan tâm | He seemed *indifferent* to the news. |

## Bài tập nhỏ

1. I felt so ______ after finishing all my deadlines this week — it was a lot to handle.
2. My colleague is always ______; he asks everyone before making a decision that affects the team.
3. She's naturally ______ — she loves meeting new people at parties.
4. I'm really ______ for everything you've done for me.
5. He can be quite ______ once he's made up his mind — hard to change his opinion.

<details>
<summary>Đáp án (bấm để xem)</summary>

1. overwhelmed / relieved (tùy ngữ cảnh — nếu vừa xong việc và thấy nhẹ nhõm thì "relieved")
2. considerate
3. outgoing
4. grateful
5. stubborn

</details>
