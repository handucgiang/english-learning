# Ngữ pháp: Câu bị động (Passive Voice)

## 1. Khi nào dùng câu bị động

- Khi không biết hoặc không quan trọng ai thực hiện hành động.
- Khi muốn nhấn mạnh vào đối tượng chịu tác động hơn là người thực hiện.
- Trong văn phong trang trọng, báo cáo, tin tức, văn bản khoa học.

## 2. Công thức chung

**S + be + V3/ed (+ by + O)**

| Thì | Chủ động | Bị động |
|---|---|---|
| Present Simple | write | am/is/are + written |
| Present Continuous | is writing | is being written |
| Past Simple | wrote | was/were written |
| Past Continuous | was writing | was being written |
| Present Perfect | has written | has been written |
| Past Perfect | had written | had been written |
| Future Simple | will write | will be written |
| Modal (can/should...) | can write | can be written |

## 3. Ví dụ chuyển đổi

- Active: They **built** this house in 1990.
  Passive: This house **was built** in 1990.

- Active: The chef **is preparing** dinner.
  Passive: Dinner **is being prepared** (by the chef).

- Active: Someone **has stolen** my bike.
  Passive: My bike **has been stolen**.

- Active: You **must submit** the form by Friday.
  Passive: The form **must be submitted** by Friday.

## 4. Khi nào giữ "by + O", khi nào bỏ

- **Bỏ "by + O"** khi người thực hiện không quan trọng hoặc hiển nhiên:
  - English is spoken in many countries. *(không cần "by people")*
- **Giữ "by + O"** khi thông tin đó quan trọng hoặc gây bất ngờ:
  - The novel was written **by a 19-year-old student**.

## 5. Lưu ý đặc biệt

- Động từ có 2 tân ngữ (give, send, offer...) có thể chuyển thành 2 câu bị động khác nhau:
  - Active: They gave **her** **a prize**.
  - Passive 1: **She** was given a prize.
  - Passive 2: **A prize** was given to her.

- Câu mệnh lệnh cũng có thể bị động hóa bằng "Let... be...":
  - Active: Clean the room!
  - Passive: Let the room be cleaned!

## 6. Bài tập

Chuyển các câu sau sang câu bị động:

1. The company launched a new product last month.
2. They are building a new bridge downtown.
3. Someone has cancelled the meeting.
4. You should finish this report by tomorrow.
5. The teacher gave the students some homework.

<summary>Đáp án</summary>

1. A new product was launched (by the company) last month.
2. A new bridge is being built downtown.
3. The meeting has been cancelled.
4. This report should be finished by tomorrow.
5. The students were given some homework. / Some homework was given to the students.
