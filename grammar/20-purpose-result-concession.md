# Ngữ pháp nâng cao: Mệnh đề Mục đích, Kết quả & Nhượng bộ

Nhóm liên từ này giúp bài viết/nói diễn đạt logic phức tạp hơn (lý do làm gì, hậu quả ra sao, dù có trở ngại gì) — rất hữu ích cho văn viết luận và giao tiếp công việc.

## 1. Mệnh đề mục đích (Purpose Clauses) — "để làm gì"

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| to + V | She left early **to catch** the train. |
| in order to + V | We arrived early **in order to get** good seats. |
| so as to + V | He whispered **so as not to wake** the baby. *(phủ định: so as not to)* |
| so that + S + can/could/will/would + V | She saved money **so that she could travel**. |
| in order that + S + can/could + V | *(trang trọng hơn "so that")* We left early **in order that we might avoid** traffic. |
| for + noun | I went to the store **for some milk**. |

**Phân biệt "to V" và "for + noun":** dùng "to V" khi diễn tả hành động, "for + noun" khi diễn tả mục đích là một vật/danh từ.
- I went there **to buy** groceries. *(không phải "for buy")*
- I went there **for groceries**.

## 2. Mệnh đề kết quả (Result Clauses) — "đến mức mà / vì vậy"

| Cấu trúc | Ví dụ |
|---|---|
| so + adj/adv + that | The exam was **so difficult that** many students failed. |
| such + (a/an) + adj + noun + that | It was **such a difficult exam that** many students failed. |
| so much/many + noun + that | There was **so much traffic that** we were an hour late. |
| ..., so | It was raining, **so** we stayed home. |
| as a result, / therefore, / consequently, | The flight was cancelled. **As a result**, we had to stay another night. |

**Phân biệt "so" và "such":**
- **so** + tính từ/trạng từ (không có danh từ theo sau): so **tired**, so **quickly**
- **such** + (a/an) + tính từ + **danh từ**: such **a tiring day**, such **good news**

## 3. Mệnh đề nhượng bộ (Concession Clauses) — "mặc dù"

| Cấu trúc | Theo sau bởi | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| although / though / even though | mệnh đề (S + V) | **Although** it was raining, we went for a walk. |
| despite / in spite of | cụm danh từ / V-ing | **Despite** the rain, we went for a walk. / **Despite** working overtime, he missed the deadline. |
| even so | đứng đầu câu độc lập, có dấu phẩy | It was raining heavily. **Even so**, we went for a walk. |
| however | đứng đầu câu độc lập, có dấu phẩy | The plan was risky. **However**, we decided to go ahead. |
| nevertheless / nonetheless | tương tự "however", trang trọng hơn | The results were disappointing. **Nevertheless**, the team kept trying. |
| no matter how/what/who | mệnh đề (S + V) | **No matter how hard** I try, I can't fix this. |

**Lưu ý phổ biến — lỗi sai của người Việt:**
- ✗ Despite of the rain, we went out. → ✓ **Despite** the rain / **In spite of** the rain *(không có "of" sau despite)*
- ✗ Although the rain, we went out. → ✓ **Although it was raining**, we went out. *(although + mệnh đề đầy đủ, không phải + danh từ)*

## 4. Bài tập

Điền từ nối phù hợp:

1. ______ he studied hard, he didn't pass the exam. (although/despite)
2. The traffic was ______ bad that we missed our flight. (so/such)
3. She saved money every month ______ she could buy a house someday. (so that/for)
4. ______ the heavy rain, the event went ahead as planned. (although/despite)
5. It was ______ a boring movie that half the audience left early. (so/such)

<details>
<summary>Đáp án</summary>

1. Although
2. so
3. so that
4. Despite
5. such

</details>
