# Ngữ pháp: So sánh (Comparatives & Superlatives)

## 1. So sánh hơn (Comparative)

### Tính từ ngắn (1 âm tiết, hoặc 2 âm tiết tận cùng -y, -er, -ow...)

**S + be + adj-er + than + O**

- tall → tall**er**, big → big**ger** *(gấp đôi phụ âm cuối)*, happy → happ**ier** *(y→i)*
- She is **taller than** me.
- This test is **easier than** the last one.

### Tính từ dài (2+ âm tiết)

**S + be + more/less + adj + than + O**

- expensive → **more** expensive
- This restaurant is **more expensive than** that one.
- His new job is **less stressful than** his old one.

### Bất quy tắc

| Tính từ | So sánh hơn |
|---|---|
| good | better |
| bad | worse |
| far | farther/further |
| little | less |
| much/many | more |

## 2. So sánh nhất (Superlative)

**S + be + the + adj-est / the most + adj**

- She is **the tallest** person in the class.
- This is **the most expensive** restaurant in town.
- That was **the best** movie I've ever seen.

## 3. So sánh bằng (Equality) & không bằng

- **as + adj + as**: She is **as tall as** her brother.
- **not as/so + adj + as**: This phone is **not as expensive as** that one.
- **the same as**: My opinion is **the same as** yours.

## 4. Nhấn mạnh mức độ so sánh

| Cụm | Ý nghĩa | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| much / a lot / far + comparative | ...hơn nhiều | This is **much better than** before. |
| a little / slightly + comparative | ...hơn một chút | She's **slightly taller than** me. |
| by far + the + superlative | vượt trội hẳn | This is **by far the best** option. |

## 5. So sánh kép (Double Comparatives) — diễn tả xu hướng thay đổi

- **The + comparative..., the + comparative...** (càng... càng...)
  - **The more** you practice, **the better** you get.
  - **The harder** you work, **the more** you achieve.

- **comparative + and + comparative** (ngày càng...)
  - The weather is getting **colder and colder**.
  - Things are becoming **more and more** difficult.

## 6. So sánh với danh từ (as many/much as, more/fewer/less)

| Loại danh từ | So sánh hơn | So sánh bằng |
|---|---|---|
| đếm được (số nhiều) | more / fewer | as many...as |
| không đếm được | more / less | as much...as |

- I have **fewer** books than she does.
- We need **less** time than expected.
- I don't have **as many** friends **as** you do.

## 7. Bài tập

Chia đúng dạng so sánh:

1. This laptop is ______ (light) than my old one.
2. She is ______ (intelligent) person I've ever met.
3. The more you read, ______ (good) your vocabulary becomes.
4. He has ______ (many) experience than the other candidates.
5. Traffic in this city is getting ______ (bad) and ______ (bad).
6. My salary is not ______ (high) as hers.

<details>
<summary>Đáp án</summary>

1. lighter
2. the most intelligent
3. the better
4. more
5. worse and worse
6. as high
