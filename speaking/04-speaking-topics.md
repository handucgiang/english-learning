# Kỹ năng Nói: Chủ đề luyện nói hằng ngày

Cách luyện: chọn 1 câu hỏi, nói to trong 1–2 phút KHÔNG chuẩn bị trước (giống phản xạ thực tế), ghi âm lại, sau đó nghe lại và tự sửa. Dần dần giảm số lần ấp úng, tăng độ trôi chảy.

## 1. Giới thiệu bản thân

1. Can you tell me about yourself?
2. What do you do for a living?
3. What are your hobbies?
4. Where are you from? What is it like there?
5. What is one thing people don't know about you?

**Khung trả lời gợi ý:** Tên/nghề nghiệp → 1-2 điều nổi bật về công việc/học tập → sở thích cá nhân → 1 câu kết mở (mời người nghe hỏi thêm).

## 2. Công việc & sự nghiệp

1. What do you like most about your job?
2. Describe a challenge you faced at work and how you solved it.
3. What are your career goals for the next five years?
4. How do you handle stress or tight deadlines?
5. Describe your ideal working environment.

## 3. Du lịch

1. What's the most memorable trip you've ever taken?
2. Do you prefer traveling alone or with others? Why?
3. What's a place you'd love to visit and why?
4. Describe your favorite way to spend a vacation.
5. What's something that surprised you while traveling?

## 4. Đời sống & quan điểm cá nhân

1. How do you usually spend your weekends?
2. What's a skill you'd like to learn and why?
3. Do you think technology has made life better or worse? Why?
4. What's a piece of advice that changed how you think?
5. How has your hometown changed over the years?

## 5. Phỏng vấn xin việc (mở rộng, xem thêm [phrases/03-interview.md](../phrases/03-interview.md))

1. Why do you want to work for this company?
2. What is your greatest strength/weakness?
3. Describe a time you worked well in a team.
4. Where do you see yourself in five years?
5. Why should we hire you?

## 6. Mẹo trả lời trôi chảy (structure: PREP)

Dùng cấu trúc **PREP** để trả lời có logic, không bị lan man:

- **P**oint — nêu ý chính ngay đầu.
- **R**eason — giải thích lý do.
- **E**xample — đưa ví dụ cụ thể.
- **P**oint (again) — chốt lại ý chính.

Ví dụ với câu "Do you prefer traveling alone or with others?":

> **Point:** I actually prefer traveling with others, especially close friends.
> **Reason:** because it's more fun to share experiences in the moment, not just look back on photos later.
> **Example:** Last year, I went hiking with two friends, and even though we got lost for an hour, it turned into one of the funniest stories we still talk about.
> **Point:** So for me, who I travel with matters more than where I go.
