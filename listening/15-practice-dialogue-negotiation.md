# Luyện nghe nâng cao: Đàm phán & Bất đồng quan điểm

**Cách dùng:** bài này khó hơn các bài luyện nghe trước — có nhiều hedging language và phản đối gián tiếp. Xem lại [12-inference-implied-meaning.md](12-inference-implied-meaning.md) trước khi nghe. Nhờ người đọc to hoặc dùng text-to-speech, cố nghe hiểu ý ẩn trước khi đọc phần giải thích bên dưới.

## Hội thoại: Thương lượng deadline dự án

> **Client (Ms. Pham):** So, I've looked over the proposal, and overall it's solid. I do have some concerns about the timeline, though.
>
> **Vendor (Alex):** Sure, I'm happy to talk through that. What's on your mind?
>
> **Ms. Pham:** Well, six weeks seems... ambitious, given the scope. I'm not saying it's impossible, but I'd feel more comfortable with a bit more buffer.
>
> **Alex:** I hear you. To be honest, six weeks is tight on our end too, but we built in some flexibility. Could I ask what's driving the concern — is it more about our track record, or the scope itself?
>
> **Ms. Pham:** A bit of both, if I'm honest. We've had timeline issues with vendors before, so I'd rather be cautious this time.
>
> **Alex:** That's fair, and I appreciate you being upfront about that. What if we did this — we deliver the core features in six weeks as planned, but treat the last two features as a second phase with its own two-week buffer? That way you get something usable on time, without us rushing the more complex parts.
>
> **Ms. Pham:** Hmm, that could work. I'd want to see a clearer breakdown of which features go into which phase, though.
>
> **Alex:** Absolutely, I can have that to you by end of day tomorrow.
>
> **Ms. Pham:** Okay. Let's go with that, tentatively — I'll confirm once I see the breakdown.
>
> **Alex:** Sounds good, thanks for working through this with me.

## Giải mã ý ẩn trong hội thoại

| Câu nói | Ý ẩn thực sự |
|---|---|
| "It's solid... I do have some concerns, though." | Bà Pham đang chuẩn bị nêu vấn đề — "though" báo hiệu phần quan trọng sắp tới. |
| "Six weeks seems... ambitious" *(ngập ngừng)* | Cách nói giảm nhẹ của "tôi nghĩ 6 tuần là không đủ/quá gấp". |
| "I'm not saying it's impossible, but..." | Hedging — chuẩn bị nêu lo ngại mà không phủ nhận thẳng thừng. |
| "A bit of both, if I'm honest." | "If I'm honest" báo hiệu bà sắp nói điều thẳng thắn hơn — thực ra có phần không tin tưởng vendor. |
| "That could work." | Đồng ý có điều kiện, KHÔNG phải đồng ý hoàn toàn — vẫn cần thêm thông tin. |
| "Let's go with that, tentatively" | "Tentatively" (tạm thời) là từ khóa quan trọng — quyết định CHƯA chốt hẳn. |

## Câu hỏi nghe hiểu

1. What is Ms. Pham's main concern about the proposal?
2. Why is Ms. Pham cautious, according to what she says?
3. What solution does Alex propose?
4. Does Ms. Pham fully agree to the solution? What condition does she set?
5. What will Alex send by when?

<details>
<summary>Đáp án</summary>

1. She's concerned the six-week timeline is too tight given the scope of the project.
2. She's had timeline issues with vendors before, so she wants to be more cautious this time.
3. Splitting the project into two phases — core features in six weeks, and the last two features in a second phase with a two-week buffer.
4. Not fully — she agrees tentatively and wants to see a clear breakdown of which features go into which phase before confirming.
5. Alex will send the breakdown by end of day tomorrow.

## Bài tập mở rộng

Nghe lại hội thoại và liệt kê tất cả các cụm hedging language xuất hiện (ví dụ: "I do have some concerns", "if I'm honest", "tentatively"...). Sau đó thử viết lại hội thoại theo cách nói THẲNG, không hedging — so sánh sự khác biệt về sắc thái giữa hai cách nói.
