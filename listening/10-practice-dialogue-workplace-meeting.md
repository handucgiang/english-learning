# Luyện nghe: Cuộc họp công sở

**Cách dùng:** xem lại chiến lược nghe trong cuộc họp tại [08-listening-in-context.md](08-listening-in-context.md) trước khi luyện bài này — chú ý các cụm báo hiệu chuyển ý và action items. Nhờ người đọc to hoặc dùng text-to-speech, sau đó làm bài tập bên dưới.

## Hội thoại: Weekly Team Meeting

> **Manager (Sarah):** Alright, everyone, let's get started. So, first item on the agenda — the Q3 report. Minh, can you give us a quick update?
>
> **Minh:** Sure. So, overall, revenue's up about twelve percent compared to last quarter, which is great. But, um, we did see a dip in the SEA region, roughly five percent down.
>
> **Sarah:** Okay, do we know why?
>
> **Minh:** It looks like it's mostly due to the delayed product launch there. We're expecting it to pick back up once that's resolved next month.
>
> **Sarah:** Got it, thanks. Moving on — the marketing campaign. Linh, where are we at with that?
>
> **Linh:** We're on track. The new campaign is set to launch next Monday. I'll need final approval on the budget by Thursday, though, or we'll have to push it back.
>
> **Sarah:** Noted. I'll get that to you by Wednesday at the latest. Anything else before we wrap up?
>
> **Minh:** Just one thing — should we follow up with the top clients in the SEA region directly, given the dip?
>
> **Sarah:** Good call. Minh, can you take the lead on that? Reach out to the top ten clients this week.
>
> **Minh:** Will do.
>
> **Sarah:** Great, thanks everyone. Same time next week.

## Ghi chú về ngôn ngữ

- "first item on the agenda" — cụm mở đầu báo hiệu chuyển sang nội dung mới
- "Moving on —" — cụm chuyển ý nhanh, rất phổ biến trong họp
- "Good call." — khen ý kiến hay/hợp lý
- "Will do." — cách xác nhận nhận việc ngắn gọn (thay cho "I will do it")

## Câu hỏi nghe hiểu

1. What happened to revenue this quarter, according to Minh?
2. Why did the SEA region see a dip in revenue?
3. What does Linh need by Thursday, and what happens if she doesn't get it?
4. What action item does Sarah assign to Minh at the end of the meeting?
5. By when will Sarah provide the budget approval?

1. Revenue is up about 12% compared to last quarter.

## Luyện ghi chú (Note-taking practice)

Trước khi xem đáp án, thử áp dụng ký hiệu ghi chú nhanh từ [03-note-taking-symbols.md](03-note-taking-symbols.md) để ghi lại các action items trong cuộc họp này khi nghe lần đầu. Ví dụ mẫu:

```
Q3 report: rev ↑12% vs Q2
  SEA ↓5% ← delayed launch, fix next mo.
Marketing: campaign launch Mon
  need budget approval b/4 Thu (Sarah → Wed)
Action: Minh → contact top 10 SEA clients this wk
```
