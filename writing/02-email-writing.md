# Kỹ năng Viết: Email công việc

## 1. Cấu trúc email chuẩn

1. **Subject line** — ngắn gọn, rõ ràng, nêu đúng nội dung chính.
2. **Lời chào (Greeting)**
3. **Câu mở đầu** — nêu lý do viết email.
4. **Nội dung chính** — chia đoạn rõ ràng nếu có nhiều ý.
5. **Câu kết** — nêu hành động mong muốn tiếp theo (call to action).
6. **Lời chào kết (Closing) + Chữ ký**

## 2. Lời chào & kết thư theo mức độ trang trọng

| Mức độ | Chào đầu | Chào kết |
|---|---|---|
| Trang trọng (chưa biết tên) | Dear Sir/Madam, | Yours faithfully, |
| Trang trọng (biết tên) | Dear Mr./Ms. [Last name], | Yours sincerely, / Best regards, |
| Bán trang trọng (đồng nghiệp) | Hi [First name], | Best regards, / Kind regards, |
| Thân mật (đồng nghiệp thân) | Hi [Name], / Hello [Name], | Thanks, / Best, |

## 3. Mẫu câu theo mục đích

### Mở đầu — nêu lý do viết
- I am writing to inquire about...
- I am writing in regard to...
- Following up on our conversation earlier, I wanted to...
- I hope this email finds you well. I'm reaching out to...

### Yêu cầu / đề nghị (Request)
- Could you please send me...?
- I would appreciate it if you could...
- Would it be possible to...?
- I was wondering if you could help with...

### Đính kèm tài liệu
- Please find attached the report you requested.
- I've attached the file for your review.

### Xin lỗi vì trễ / vấn đề
- I apologize for the delay in responding.
- I'm sorry for any inconvenience this may have caused.
- Please accept my apologies for the confusion.

### Nhắc lại / theo dõi (Follow-up)
- I just wanted to follow up on my previous email.
- Just a gentle reminder about...
- I haven't heard back from you regarding..., so I wanted to check in.

### Kết thúc, yêu cầu hành động
- Please let me know if you have any questions.
- I look forward to hearing from you.
- Please confirm at your earliest convenience.
- Looking forward to your reply.

## 4. Mẫu email hoàn chỉnh

### Email yêu cầu thông tin (trang trọng)

```
Subject: Inquiry About Product Availability

Dear Ms. Johnson,

I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to inquire about the availability
of the XR-200 model before placing a bulk order.

Could you please confirm the current stock level and the estimated delivery
time for an order of 500 units?

I would appreciate a response at your earliest convenience.

Best regards,
Nguyen Van A
Sales Department, ABC Company
```

### Email xin nghỉ phép (bán trang trọng)

```
Subject: Leave Request - March 10-12

Hi Sarah,

I'd like to request three days of leave from March 10th to March 12th for a
family matter. I will make sure all my current tasks are handed over to
Minh before I leave.

Please let me know if this works, or if you need anything from me before then.

Thanks,
Linh
```

## 5. Bài tập

Viết một email (5–7 câu) gửi cho quản lý để báo cáo rằng bạn sẽ hoàn thành báo cáo trễ 1 ngày so với deadline, kèm lý do và đề xuất mốc thời gian mới.

```
Subject: Slight Delay on the Quarterly Report

Hi [Manager's name],

I wanted to give you a heads-up that the quarterly report will be about
a day later than planned. We ran into a data issue from the finance team
that took longer to resolve than expected.

I expect to have the final version to you by end of day Thursday instead
of Wednesday. Please let me know if this causes any issues on your end.

Thanks for your understanding,
[Your name]
```
