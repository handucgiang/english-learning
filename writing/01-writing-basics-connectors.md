# Kỹ năng Viết: Nền tảng & Từ nối câu

## 1. Lỗi phổ biến của người Việt khi viết tiếng Anh

| Lỗi | Sai | Đúng |
|---|---|---|
| Thiếu chủ ngữ giả "it" | Is raining today. | **It** is raining today. |
| Thiếu mạo từ (a/an/the) | I bought book yesterday. | I bought **a** book yesterday. |
| Sai trật tự tính từ | a car red new | a **new red** car (thứ tự: ý kiến → kích thước → tuổi → hình dạng → màu → nguồn gốc → chất liệu → danh từ) |
| Dịch trực tiếp cấu trúc Việt sang Anh | I very like it. | I like it **very much**. |
| Không chia động từ theo chủ ngữ số ít/nhiều | He like coffee. | He **likes** coffee. |
| Dùng sai giới từ | good in English | good **at** English |
| Câu quá dài, không dấu câu | I went to the store I bought milk I came home | I went to the store, bought milk, and came home. |

## 2. Từ nối câu (Linking Words / Connectors)

### Thêm ý (Addition)
- in addition, furthermore, moreover, besides, also, not only... but also...

> The hotel is affordable. **Moreover**, it's located right in the city center.

### Đối lập (Contrast)
- however, on the other hand, nevertheless, although, despite, in contrast, whereas

> **Although** the test was difficult, most students passed.
> The city is crowded. **However**, it has a great public transport system.

### Nguyên nhân – kết quả (Cause & Effect)
- because, since, as, due to, therefore, as a result, consequently, so

> The flight was delayed **due to** bad weather. **As a result**, we missed our connection.

### Ví dụ (Example)
- for example, for instance, such as, namely

> Many countries have adopted remote work policies, **such as** Vietnam, Singapore, and Japan.

### Kết luận / Tóm tắt (Conclusion)
- in conclusion, to sum up, overall, in short, all in all

> **In conclusion**, both options have advantages, but the second one suits our budget better.

### Trình tự thời gian (Sequence)
- first(ly), then, next, after that, finally, meanwhile, subsequently

## 3. Câu ghép & câu phức (Compound & Complex Sentences)

Viết chỉ toàn câu đơn ngắn khiến văn bản rời rạc, thiếu tự nhiên. Kết hợp câu bằng liên từ hoặc mệnh đề quan hệ giúp bài viết mạch lạc hơn.

- **Câu đơn:** I finished the report. I sent it to my manager.
- **Câu ghép:** I finished the report, **and** I sent it to my manager.
- **Câu phức:** **After** I finished the report, I sent it to my manager.
- Dùng mệnh đề quan hệ: The report, **which took two days to finish**, was sent to my manager.

## 4. Bài tập

Nối các câu sau bằng từ nối phù hợp:

1. The project was over budget. ___, we decided to cut some features.
2. She studies hard. ___, she doesn't always get good grades.
3. He was tired. ___, he kept working until midnight.
4. There are many benefits to remote work, ___ flexibility and reduced commuting time.

<details>
<summary>Đáp án gợi ý</summary>

1. As a result / Therefore
2. However / Nevertheless
3. Despite that / Nevertheless (or: **Although** he was tired, he kept working until midnight.)
4. such as / for example
